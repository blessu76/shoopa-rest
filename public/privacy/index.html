Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Shoopa app and its official website handle data.

On this website

The website stores your language preference in your browser. You can clear it in your browser settings. GitHub Pages hosts this site and records and stores visitors’ IP addresses in access logs for security.

Data stored on your device

On your device, the app stores the highest level you’ve reached, scores for leaderboard submission, and whether the Moonlight Pack is unlocked and selected.

Cloud saves and leaderboards

When platform services are connected, the app syncs your highest level through iCloud on iOS or the Google Play Games Saved Games service on Android. The app submits leaderboard scores separately to Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. Other players may see your platform profile name and scores on leaderboards.

In-app purchases

The App Store or Google Play processes payments and keeps purchase records for the optional, non-consumable Moonlight Pack. The app checks purchase status to unlock or restore the pack. Card numbers are not sent to ipe Studio.
