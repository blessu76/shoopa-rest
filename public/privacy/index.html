Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Shoopa app and its official website handle data.

On this website

The website stores your language preference in your browser. You can clear it in your browser settings.

Cloud saves and leaderboards

When cloud features are used, platform services store progress and leaderboard data. On iOS, iCloud stores progress and Game Center handles Highest Stage and Daily Best Combo records. On Android, Google Play Games stores progress and leaderboard data. Other players may see your platform profile name and scores on leaderboards. You can manage saved data and visibility through your platform account.

In-app purchases

The App Store or Google Play processes payments and keeps purchase records for the optional, non-consumable Moonlight Pack. The app checks purchase status to unlock or restore the pack. Card numbers are not sent to ipe Studio.
