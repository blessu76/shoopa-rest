How the arrows formed a heart

The 4A play mockup shows a heart of arrows on a 5×5 grid, alongside the 0.9-second combo timer.

Shoopa starts on a 5×5 board. For the 4A play mockup, we gave each arrow direction its own color and arranged the blocks into a heart.

Remove another block within 0.9 seconds to keep the combo going. A blocked tap breaks the combo and costs a heart. If you run out of hearts, you can retry the same board. In the 4A mockup, we showed the remaining block count, hearts, and combo on one screen.
