Arrow Tap Puzzle

Shoopa

Find the path. Tap it clear.

Tap an arrow block when its path is open. Work out the right order and clear the board, one block at a time.

How to play

How a round works

  • ↗

    Find an open path

    A block can leave when every space in front of its arrow is clear to the edge.

  • ϟ

    Keep the combo going

    Remove another block within 0.9 seconds to keep the combo going. A blocked tap breaks the combo.

  • ♥

    Three hearts, two hints

    Tap a blocked arrow and you lose a heart. You can retry the same board for free.

Launch plans

At launch, you’ll be able to play boards from 5×5 to 9×9 without ads. We’re preparing the Best Level and Daily Best Combo leaderboards. Daily Best Combo will count only attempts that reach the result screen without using hints.
